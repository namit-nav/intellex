# Intellex — AI Research Intelligence Platform

## Overview

Intellex is an AI-powered research intelligence platform developed to streamline company analysis, strategic planning, document understanding, and competitive research through a modern full-stack web application.

The platform combines advanced AI workflows with an interactive user experience to help users generate research reports, analyze documents, compare companies, and create strategic plans using natural language interactions.

---

## Core Features

### Company Research
Generate detailed AI-powered reports on companies including:
- Market positioning
- Strategic insights
- Products & services
- Competitive analysis
- Executive summaries

### Strategic Planner
Convert ideas, goals, or problems into structured execution roadmaps and strategic plans.

### DocsTool — Document Intelligence
Upload PDF documents and ask contextual questions based on the uploaded content.

### Company Comparison
Compare two companies side-by-side using AI-generated analysis and insights.

### Contact System
Integrated contact functionality with real-time feedback handling.

## How Intellex Works

Intellex uses AI-powered workflows to process user queries, research companies, analyze uploaded documents, and generate structured outputs in real time.

The frontend communicates with FastAPI backend services which interact with AI models, document parsers, and research pipelines to deliver intelligent responses and reports.
